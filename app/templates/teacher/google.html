First time? Set up your sender (about 5 minutes, once)

This runs in your own Google account with Apps Script. You don't need Google Cloud.

  1. Go to script.google.com while signed in to your school account. A new project opens.
  2. Delete what's in Code.gs. and paste it in.
  3. On the left, press + next to Services. Choose Google Classroom API, then Add.
  4. Press Deploy, then New deployment. Click the gear and pick Web app.
  5. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Only myself. Press Deploy, then allow access when Google asks.
  6. Copy the Web app URL into step 2 above.

If Google says the app is blocked or unverified, your school's Google admin may need to allow Apps Script to use Classroom.

How do I get my code onto the micro:bit?
  1. Plug the micro:bit into the computer with a USB cable.
  2. First time only: click Download .hex and drag the file onto the MICROBIT drive. The yellow light blinks while it copies. This installs Python on the micro:bit.
  3. Click Send to micro:bit. The first time, pick “BBC micro:bit” in the pop-up and click Connect.
  4. Your program runs right away. Anything you print() — and any error messages — show up under the editor.

Send to micro:bit needs Chrome, Edge or a Chromebook. In other browsers, use Download .hex every time.
